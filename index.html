About Us

More than a community.

Salaah began with a simple idea: that the best learning happens when students teach each other. We are a space where curiosity is encouraged, questions are welcomed, and every member has something valuable to share. Through mentorship, collaboration, and honest conversations, we help each other build confidence, sharpen skills, and grow — not just as engineers, but as thoughtful individuals ready to contribute meaningfully.

01

Learn Together

Knowledge grows when shared. We create an environment where peers teach, question, and explore ideas together — making learning a collective journey rather than a solitary task.

02

Build Together

From projects to events, we collaborate to create things that matter. Working side by side, we develop not just technical skills, but the ability to communicate, plan, and execute as a team.

03

Grow Together

Personal growth is at the heart of Salaah. We support each other through challenges, celebrate progress, and build a network of peers who inspire one another to become better versions of themselves.

Our Teams

Departments

Each department plays a vital role in keeping Salaah running, growing, and connected with the student community.

Technical Operations

Handles the technical backbone of Salaah — managing digital platforms, building tools, maintaining websites, and ensuring smooth execution of tech-driven initiatives and events.

Planning & Strategy

Designs the roadmap for community activities, coordinates timelines, aligns goals across teams, and ensures every initiative contributes to Salaah's long-term vision.

Social Media & Marketing

Manages Salaah's online presence — creating engaging content, building brand identity, and ensuring the community's voice reaches students across campus and beyond.

Content & Documentation

Crafts written and visual content — from event write-ups and newsletters to guides and resources — preserving the knowledge and stories that define Salaah's journey.

Outreach & Community

Focuses on building relationships — connecting with students, faculty, and external communities to expand Salaah's reach and create meaningful partnerships.

What We Do

Programs & Events

Salaah organizes a range of programs designed to foster learning, collaboration, and skill development. These are illustrative categories of activities our community engages in.

01

Mentorship Sessions

One-on-one and group mentoring where senior students guide juniors through academics, projects, career paths, and personal growth — creating a supportive learning chain.

02

Technical Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering programming, design, tools, and emerging technologies — led by community members who are passionate about teaching what they know.

03

Community Meetups

Regular informal gatherings where members share ideas, discuss interests, network, and strengthen the bonds that make Salaah more than just a club.

04

Skill-Building Activities

Focused activities around communication, leadership, teamwork, and creative thinking — helping members develop the soft skills that complement technical knowledge.
